# 📘 TwoAxis Finance Docs

Welcome to the TwoAxis Finance documentation. This guide explains the key financial concepts used in the app and how different entries interact with the **Transactions** list — your master record of all money flow.

---

## 💼 Core Concepts

### 🟢 Asset
An **asset** is something you own that has value. This could be cash, a bank account balance, crypto holdings, etc.

- Example: EGP 5,000 in your Banque Misr account.

---

### 🔴 Liability
A **liability** is money you owe to others — loans, credit card debt, or anything you’re expected to pay in the future.

- Example: EGP 10,000 owed on your student loan.

> ✅ When you **make a payment** toward a liability, it gets recorded in the **Transactions** list.

---

### 🟡 Receivable
A **receivable** is money that someone owes you. This could be a loan you gave a friend, unpaid freelance work, or anything expected to come in.

- Example: EGP 1,200 someone owes you for splitting rent.

> ✅ When you **receive a receivable** (i.e., they pay you back), it gets recorded as a **Transaction**.

---

### 🟣 Income
**Income** is any money that you earn — salary, freelance payments, investments, etc.

- Example: Monthly salary of EGP 7,000.

> ✅ When you **get paid**, a **Transaction** is automatically created.

---

### 🔵 Bills
A **bill** is a **recurring expense** you pay regularly — rent, internet, gym, etc.

- Example: EGP 1,500/month for rent.

> ✅ Every time a bill is paid, it shows up in **Transactions**.

---

### 🟠 Expense
An **expense** is a **one-time cost** — food, transportation, shopping, etc.

- Example: EGP 250 on groceries.

> ✅ Adding a one-time expense also adds a new **Transaction**.

---

## 🔄 Transactions: Your Money Flow Log

The **Transactions** list is where all the action happens. It records **every inflow and outflow** of money. Here’s what automatically adds a transaction:

| Action                          | Adds Transaction? |
|---------------------------------|-------------------|
| Paying a liability              | ✅ Yes             |
| Receiving a receivable          | ✅ Yes             |
| Getting paid (income)           | ✅ Yes             |
| Paying a bill                   | ✅ Yes             |
| Adding a one-time expense       | ✅ Yes             |

Each transaction includes the amount, category, date, and the related account/entry.

---

## 📎 Example Transaction Flow

> You pay EGP 1,000 toward your student loan (a liability):  
➡️ A transaction is created showing EGP -1,000

> Your friend pays you back EGP 300 (a receivable):  
➡️ A transaction is created showing EGP +300

> You log an expense of EGP 150 on food:  
➡️ A transaction is created showing EGP -150
