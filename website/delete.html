Delete your account

You can easily delete your account and all of its data in TwoAxis Finance by following these steps:

  1. Login to your account if you haven't already.
  2. In the top left corner, press on the three dots.
  3. Choose Settings from the drop-down menu.
  4. Press on Delete your account.
  5. Enter your password.
  6. Click Delete.
